The idea / at a glance

A clearer picture
of burner safety.

AGNI KAVACH explores one proposed chain: monitor conditions around a hot-air balloon burner, compare sensor inputs against predefined limits, then signal an alert and close a normally closed solenoid valve when the concept calls for intervention.

It is a learning model, not a validated design. The page describes the intended relationships among parts—not an installed system, flight procedure, test result or guarantee of protection.
